  • Beta-Sitosterol: This plant sterol is crucial for maintaining a healthy urinary flow and reducing the urgency and frequency of urination. Research suggests that beta-sitosterol can improve urinary symptoms and flow rates in men with BPH.
  • Zinc: An essential mineral, zinc plays a critical role in prostate function and health. Studies have shown that prostate tissue contains high levels of zinc, and a deficiency can be linked to prostate issues.
