Prodentim works by introducing a potent dose of good bacteria directly into your mouth. These beneficial strains help to:

  • **Promote Fresh Breath:** By neutralizing odor-causing bacteria.
  • **Reduce Inflammation:** Contributing to healthier gums and preventing conditions like gingivitis.
The chewable format of Prodentim is crucial, as it allows the probiotics to directly colonize the mouth, maximizing their effectiveness compared to swallowed capsules that primarily target the gut.
