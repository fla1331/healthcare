What is Prodentim?

Prodentim is an oral probiotic supplement designed to support dental health by introducing beneficial bacteria into your mouth. Unlike conventional oral care products that focus on eliminating bacteria, Prodentim takes a different approach by replenishing your oral microbiome with specific probiotic strains selected for their dental health benefits.

How Prodentim Works

The supplement works by delivering billions of colony-forming units (CFUs) of probiotics directly to your oral cavity. These beneficial bacteria help restore balance to your mouth's microbiome, supporting natural defenses against harmful bacteria that can cause dental issues. By maintaining this balance, Prodentim supports overall oral wellness from within.

Key Ingredients and Their Benefits

Prodentim contains a specialized blend of probiotic strains scientifically selected for oral health:

  • Lactobacillus Paracasei: Supports gum health and helps maintain healthy oral flora balance
  • B.lactis BL-04: Contributes to immune function in the oral cavity
  • Lactobacillus Reuteri: Helps maintain dental wellness and supports fresh breath
  • BLIS K-12: Specifically studied for oral health benefits and fresh breath support
  • BLIS M-18: Research suggests benefits for dental plaque management
